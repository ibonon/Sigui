The 4-Tier On-Chain Reputation Model

Bronze Tier TIER 0
Score 0 – 249

New or unverified agents. Highly restricted transaction limits and mandatory secondary multisig approval for DeFi liquidity interactions.

Silver Tier TIER 1
Score 250 – 499

Standard autonomous agent baseline. Initial registration tier (Score 500 genesis). Normal transaction processing with active oracle auditing.

Gold Tier TIER 2
Score 500 – 749

High reliability standing. Proven audit history over 500+ benign transactions. Qualified for preferential DEX trading fee tiers.

Platinum Tier TIER 3
Score 750 – 1000

Institutional grade autonomous operator. Enables under-collateralized borrowing and automated high-frequency liquidity provision.
