Attack Taxonomy

The 3 Recognized Adversarial Patterns

🚨 DRAIN_STAR HIGH SEVERITY

A malicious sink contract receives rapid, concurrent fund transfers from multiple victims (agents, treasuries) in a star fan-in pattern, followed by immediate exit sweeps.

Graph Signature High in-degree, low persistence, rapid dissipation
⚡ MIXING_CHAIN SUSPICIOUS

Sequential multi-hop fund splitting through intermediary relay contracts or unverified cross-chain bridges designed to obfuscate origin ownership from rule-based filters.

Graph Signature Path length >= 4, peeling chain, entropy > 0.85
🕸️ COORD_CLUSTER ANOMALY

Sybil swarms executing front-running, sandwich attacks, or liquidity drain coordination against agent rebalancing orders across decentralized exchanges.

Graph Signature Dense k-clique clustering, microsecond clustering
