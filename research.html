Scientific Foundations

Key Research Findings from the Paper

1. Visual Adjacency Encoding PILLAR 1

Converting on-chain transaction flows into 2D adjacency visual matrices allows vision transformers to recognize global structural invariants. This bypasses address randomization, proxy upgrades, and bytecode obfuscation that blind static scanners.

2. Hardware-Pinned Weights PILLAR 2

The 192GB HBM3 memory bandwidth on the AMD Instinct MI300X enables full FP16 model weights to remain pinned in memory. This eliminates KV cache page swapping and enables sustained sub-40ms execution under concurrent agent loads.

3. Groth16 Verifier Invariant PILLAR 3

Generating cryptographic witnesses over the BN128 scalar field allows on-chain smart contracts on Aptos and Ethereum to verify model classifications in under 5,000 gas units, ensuring zero leak of private agent trade parameters.
